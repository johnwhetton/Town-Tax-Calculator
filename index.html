The original proposed mill rate was 26.44, then adjusted to 26.02 before the first referendum due to unexpected state revenue. After the first referendum failed, the proposed mill rate was adjusted to 25.66. The second referendum resulted in 813 yes and 1,103 no and was defeated. The proposed mill rate was then adjusted to 25.59 for the third referendum, held on June 23, 2026, which resulted in 795 yes and 957 no and was defeated. The fourth referendum is scheduled for July 29, 2026, with a proposed mill rate of 25.35.
